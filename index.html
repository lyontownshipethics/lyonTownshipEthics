A citizen-maintained public archive

Trust But Verify: An independent record of citizen-government communications

Preserving primary-source correspondence connected to Lyon Township government decisions—so the public record remains accessible, reviewable, and complete.

Why this exists

Public decisions deserve a public record.

This independent archive documents communications between citizens and Lyon Township government officials.

Materials are presented as primary sources wherever possible, with enough context to understand when they were sent, who received them, and which public matter they concern.

This site is not an official publication of Lyon Township. Its purpose is preservation and public accountability—not to replace the township’s official records.

Publication standards

A record readers can evaluate for themselves.

01

Primary sources

Original letters, emails, and public documents are published with minimal alteration.

02

Clear context

Each record identifies its date, sender, recipients, and relationship to township business.

03

Transparent corrections

Material changes and corrections will be noted rather than silently replacing the record.
