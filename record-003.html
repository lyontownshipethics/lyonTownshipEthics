Environmental Noise, Low-Frequency Sound, Infrasound, and Vibration Control Ordinance

Model draft for Lyon Township Oakland County, Michigan 7 pages
Draft proposal — not enacted law. The document contains legislative placeholders and states that local counsel and acoustical review are required.

Proposal summary

Source-neutral standards for stationary noise and vibration

The proposal addresses environmental noise, low-frequency sound, infrasound, resonance, and vibration. It includes receiving-property limits, measurement and attribution procedures, review requirements for major stationary sources, monitoring, complaint investigation, corrective action, and enforcement provisions.
